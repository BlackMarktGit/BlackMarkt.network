### Diligence Compendium Summary

In corporate divestitures, technology debt represents the single largest source of post-signing purchase price friction. This benchmark examines 140 mega-cap transactions closed between 2022 and 2025, quantifying how unmitigated IT entanglement triggers average TSA fee overruns of 34% and delays clean sponsor exits by up to 14 months.
