### Executive Abstract

The rapid decentralization of artificial intelligence compute and sovereign data requirements has created structural deficits in global power grid allocations and fiber redundancy. 

This research dossier evaluates 24 sovereign jurisdictions across three core dimensions: energy availability, geopolitical routing resilience, and legal protection from foreign discovery subpoenas under Swiss and English law.
