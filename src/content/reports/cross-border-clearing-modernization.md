### Financial Infrastructure Whitepaper

Cross-border payments remain encumbered by legacy correspondent banking networks and multi-day settlement friction. This study documents the migration paths available to global financial consortia seeking sub-second deterministic finality while satisfying stringent Basel IV capital adequacy mandates.
