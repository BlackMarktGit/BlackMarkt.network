### The End of Multiple Arbitrage

For over a decade, private equity firms relied on multiple expansion and leverage to generate outsized returns. As central banks maintain disciplined baseline rates, EBITDA growth through true operational efficiency has replaced financial engineering as the sole driver of top-quartile performance.

```
Valuation Protection Protocol:
1. Pre-Signing Forensic Code Audit (14-Day Sprint)
2. Day-1 TSA Decoupling Roadmap
3. Capital Structure Hedging against Rate Volatility
```

### Forensic Due Diligence as Value Creation

When evaluating portfolio assets, technical debt is no longer an engineering complaint—it is an unhedged financial liability. Target companies burdened by monolith architectures spend up to 45% of annual engineering budgets on basic system maintenance rather than competitive differentiation. 

By identifying these drags prior to definitive transaction agreements, acquiring institutions routinely negotiate purchase price reductions or holdbacks that directly protect IRR targets.
